IMPLEMENTATIONS AND ATTRIBUTION

All comparison rules in this repository were implemented for this study.
They are not official software implementations supplied by the authors of
structural EM, EMDL or FAB.

Method                         Source in runtime/comparisons/
Screening and cutoff choices   fixed_selection.py
Top-r families per component   fixed_selection.py
Top-M score-ranked combinations fixed_selection.py
Matched-budget random search   fixed_selection.py
Beam search                    adaptive.py (replay_beam)
Greedy family updating         adaptive.py
Constrained fixed-K FAB        fab.py
Study execution and accounting worker.py
Paired/joint-K aggregation     report.py

The top-r, top-M, random and beam methods compare fully fitted mixtures by
BIC. Greedy updating is our specified BIC procedure, related to the general
structural-EM idea; it is not a complete implementation of every structural
EM algorithm. EMDL is not implemented in this study.

The FAB comparison follows the family-update approach of Fujimaki and
Morinaga (2012), using its own approximate Bayesian objective. It is adapted
to a fixed component count, the thirteen FMVMM families and common numerical
parameter restrictions. It does not delete components. Comparing K uses the
FAB objective, not BIC; BIC is evaluated afterward. Results with an unfinished
K fit must not be interpreted as complete comparisons across K.

Fujimaki, R. and Morinaga, S. (2012). Factorized asymptotic Bayesian inference
for mixture modeling. Proceedings of AISTATS, PMLR 22, 400-408.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v22/fujimaki12.html

The numerical FMVMM package is the exact development-source snapshot used
for the experiments, commit c40dc363f4651e62681bea5420507cebc4bb3b16. It is
included under runtime/base/native/fmvmm with its original MIT license.
Do not substitute a current PyPI version and assume it is the same numerical
implementation. Portable entrypoints change paths and execution/integrity
checks; the archived numerical algorithms remain unchanged.
